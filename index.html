2.3.0

Wildlands: 20 mechs, one 3D world

A new 20-pilot Showdown with towns, forests and mountains, jumping and no minimap. Plus free mechs for playtime, bigger random maps, a 10-pilot party lobby with emotes and voice lines.

2.2.2

New touch controls, smoother difficulty

Redrawn glass touch controls, 10 hidden difficulty levels that rise with your trophies, and new pilots now start in the lobby.

2.2.1

Progress sync fix

Purchases no longer disappear between devices: progress now merges across PC, phone and browser. New LINK DEVICES in Settings connects CrazyGames too.

2.2

Holoforge, the Illusionist

A new Legendary mech that turns enemies into holograms and fills the arena with real and fake Bastions. Plus a 7-day login calendar and a faster start for new pilots.

2.1

Squad Cubes

In Duo and Trio Showdown, Power Cubes now power up your whole squad, with no 12-cube cap. Plus a new cube look, Fusion picker and Settings fixes.

2.0.2

Basic training & heavier hitters

New pilots get a guided training match, a tour of the hangar and tips in their first matches. Every mech hits about 16% harder, and Bastion about 45%.

The game

Built for quick, loud fights

The Titan Circuit is an industrial ring world at war. The Ironvale Compact fights the corrupted Hollow Choir for its foundries, and every battle is fought by mechs.

⚙

40 unique mechs

Seven rarities, from Common to Ultra Legendary and Elemental. No two share an attack or a dash.

⚔

Every way to fight

1v1 Duel, 2v2, 3v3, Showdown, Titan Fusion, Ranked from Bronze to Unmatched, and PvE contracts.

⇄

True cross-play

Windows, Android and browser players meet in the same matches, with parties, friends and cloud saves.

★

Real progression

Trophy road, power levels, Forged Rewards, daily quests, a season pass, skins and signature gears.

◎

Hidden pilots

Godly pilots fight on foot and call in their own mega mech when the fight turns.

✋

Play your way

Keyboard and mouse, a controller, or twin-stick touch controls on your phone.
