What's new

Latest updates

See every release and its patch notes →

2.7.0

Flight Protocol

Mechs can fly. Race four sky routes through rings, hazards and drones, then chase the Sky Leviathan and destroy it from the inside. Earn pilot ranks from Bronze to Titan and unlock engine trails.

2.6.1

Faster fights

The whole game is faster: quicker mechs, faster shots, sooner dashes and a 5-second respawn. Matches end in about 90 seconds, and the four elementals got 45% more hull and damage.

2.6.0

Design your own mech

Own every mech and you can build your own: pick a body, attack, heavy weapon, dash and super from any mech, name it, then upgrade, evolve and take it online.

2.5.0

Evolution

Gears are gone: every mech now evolves from V1 to V4. Beat your mech's Evolution Trial, four islands of its own echoes and a boss, to unlock the next form.

2.4.1

All 43 mechs rebuilt

Brand-new detailed models for every mech, with face screens, real metal shine and better firing animations.

2.4.0

Map creator and practice

Build and share your own arenas, practice with any mech, and send friends a link straight into your match.

2.3.0

Wildlands: 20 mechs, one 3D world

A new 20-pilot Showdown in three 3D worlds, starting from a dropship. Mechs with face screens, better graphics, free mechs for playtime, bigger random maps, a 10-pilot party lobby with emotes and voice lines.

2.2.2

New touch controls, smoother difficulty

Redrawn glass touch controls, 10 hidden difficulty levels that rise with your trophies, and new pilots now start in the lobby.

The game

Built for quick, loud fights

The Titan Circuit is an industrial ring world at war. The Ironvale Compact fights the corrupted Hollow Choir for its foundries, and every battle is fought by mechs.

⚙

40 unique mechs

Seven rarities, from Common to Ultra Legendary and Elemental. No two share an attack or a dash.

⚔

Every way to fight

1v1 Duel, 2v2, 3v3, Showdown, Titan Fusion, Ranked from Bronze to Unmatched, and PvE contracts.

⇄

True cross-play

Windows, Android and browser players meet in the same matches, with parties, friends and cloud saves.

★

Real progression

Trophy road, power levels, Forged Rewards, daily quests, a season pass, skins and signature gears.

◎

Hidden pilots

Godly pilots fight on foot and call in their own mega mech when the fight turns.

✋

Play your way

Keyboard and mouse, a controller, or twin-stick touch controls on your phone.
